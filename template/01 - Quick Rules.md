## Voice

<!-- option "Third person" -->

3. **Keep it simple.** Short sentences, common words, no idioms (§06, §09).

## Links, images, and safety

13. **Use descriptive link text:** "See the expense policy," not "click here" (§04).
14. **Add alt text to images,** and never use color as the only way to show meaning (§07).
15. **Never put passwords or customer data in docs.** Say where to find them instead (§10).
