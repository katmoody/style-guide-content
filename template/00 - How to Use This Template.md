# 00 - How to use this template

*Internal Documentation Style Guide Template, created by Katrina Moody. Licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).*

> **This page is for whoever sets up your team's guide.** Read it, use it, then delete it. It doesn't belong in your published guide.

## Why a template for internal docs?

Most style guide examples are written for public content: marketing sites, brand voice, product UI. Internal docs have different problems. They're written by people whose main job isn't writing, they live in several tools at once, and they go stale fast.

A copy-and-paste style guide won't fix that. For internal docs, you also have to consider your team's culture and how your team actually works. This template gives you a solid starting point, and the questionnaire helps you make it yours.

## How to build your guide

1. **Fill out the questionnaire** (the Style Guide Questionnaire for Internal Teams, included with this template) with the people who write your docs. Plan about an hour.
2. **Pick your sections.** In questionnaire question A8, mark each section **Now**, **Later**, or **Skip**. Start with the Now sections. A good first version beats a perfect one that never ships.
3. **Customize each section** using your answers (see below).
4. **Delete every template note,** including this page.
5. **Run the checklist in §02** one last time, then share your guide (see questionnaire question E6).

## How to customize

The template uses three kinds of markers:

- **[Text in brackets]** is a placeholder. Replace it with your team's information: "[Company]" becomes your company name.
- **Customize notes** look like this:

  > **Customize (B5):** If you chose third person, flip these examples.

  The code in parentheses is the questionnaire question that decides that rule. Make the change, then delete the note.
- **Template notes** appear at the top of each section:

  > **Template note:** Notes like this are instructions for whoever sets up the guide. **Delete them before you publish.**

**Before you publish, search the whole guide for "Customize" and "Template note," and scan for any [bracketed placeholders].** If you find any, the guide isn't finished yet.

## A few tips

- **Every team is different.** What works for one may not work for another. Change anything that doesn't fit, and cut anything you don't need.
- **Name an owner** (questionnaire question A2). A guide nobody owns is one that goes stale.
- **Share it widely.** Link it from your doc templates, onboarding, and team channels so people actually find it.
